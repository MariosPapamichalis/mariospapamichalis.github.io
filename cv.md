## In brief

**Appointments.** Postdoctoral Associate, Research Fellow and Affiliated Researcher, Human
Nature Lab and Yale Institute for Network Science, Yale University (2021&ndash;present).
Postdoctoral Researcher, Temple University (2020&ndash;2021). Postdoctoral Researcher,
Purdue University (2019&ndash;2020).

**Education.** MPhil and PhD in Statistics, University College London (2019). MSc in Machine
Learning and Computational Statistics, University College London (2012). MEng and BEng in
Electrical and Computer Engineering, National Technical University of Athens (2010).

**Selected honours.** Best Paper Award, SSDBM 2012. Gold Medal, Balkan Mathematical Olympiad,
2005. Bronze Medal, International Mathematical Olympiad, 2004.

**Technical.** Python, R, Stan, Julia, MATLAB, C/C++, Java, SQL, JavaScript. Bayesian
modelling, MCMC, variational inference, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch. Hadoop, Spark,
high-performance computing clusters.

**Languages.** English (fluent), Greek (native), Spanish (professional working proficiency).
